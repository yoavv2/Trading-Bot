<objective>
Expose the Data -> Decide -> Trade -> Sync -> Verify pipeline per evaluation session and the execution operation with its intents (OPR-03, D-04): bounded, read-only, honest about unknown calendar, with historical sessions never executable.

This plan edits only its own service, its own route module (the empty router 21-02 mounted) and its own tests. Stage semantics come from 21-02's `derive_stage_statuses`; calendar facts, owner and permission come from 21-02's shared facts; the job-type attribution comes from the core mapping. It imports no other wave-3 module (not 21-05's coverage service, not 21-03's issues).

Purpose: the operator must see why a session did or did not trade and what the next safe action is, from a frozen per-session record, without the frontend interpreting job lists (02 sec.9). A paused operation must show the unsent intents it preserved (Phase 20.1 D-16..D-20).
Output: sessions service, two GET endpoints, pipeline/executability/operation tests, 29 Sep fixture pins.
</objective>
