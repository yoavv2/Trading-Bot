# Phase 20.1 Plan 10: Uncertain-Outcome Recovery Summary

**One domain predicate now decides whether a strategy's ambiguous order outcomes are resolved. Every paper-session submission (fresh or retry) is refused with a typed 409 while they are not. Broker sync is never gated and never resolves anything. A never-found order stays unresolved until the broker shows it or its own attempt history proves it was never sent. A broker statement of non-receipt is audited evidence only.**
