<objective>
Close REVIEW SAF-03 (medium: Continue never asserts that the sent identity equals the pinned identity) and SAF-06 (low: an exception after the ACCEPTED outcome is recorded is treated as a clean failure).

Output: the identity assertion with a re-evaluation outcome, view-built OrderIntent, typed ambiguity for unparseable 2xx replies, tests.
</objective>
