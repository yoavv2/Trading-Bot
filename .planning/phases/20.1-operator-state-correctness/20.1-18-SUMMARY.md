# Phase 20.1 Plan 18: Attempt log append-only in the database and advisory-lock cleanup Summary

**Migration 0028 makes order_submission_attempts append-only/complete-once with a BEFORE UPDATE OR DELETE trigger and a RESTRICT paper-order FK, and session_run_lock now always closes its connection and invalidates it when pg_advisory_unlock fails (SAF-10, SAF-12).**

## Performance

- **Tasks:** 2 of 2
- **Files:** 2 created, 5 modified (plus this SUMMARY)

## Accomplishments

- A completed `ambiguous` attempt can no longer be rewritten to `pre_connection`; no attempt row (complete or incomplete) can be deleted; an incomplete row's identity/fencing columns (started_at, attempt_number, execution_epoch, executor_job_id, authorization_deadline, paper_order_id) are immutable; a row completes once.
- Deleting a paper order that has attempt evidence fails, and the attempts and the `execution_operation_intents.paper_order_id` link survive; an order without attempts still deletes.
- Both production completion paths stay legal under the trigger (proved by the existing `test_order_submission_attempts.py` / `test_execution_operations.py` suites and the new `test_complete_attempt_late_succeeds_under_the_trigger`).
- SAF-12: unlock failure can neither leak the (strategy, session) lock nor hide the guarded body's exception.
