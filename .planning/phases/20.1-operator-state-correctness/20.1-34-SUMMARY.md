### Harness and wording adjustments (inside the plan's own files)

**2. [Rule 1 - test design] Basis arrangement of the new-Start tests (Tests B and D, and the b3-session test in Task 3)**
- **Plan text:** `evaluation(..., base=<WallClockTimeline instant after the release>)`.
- **Problem (reproduced, see Mutation sanity):** `seed_verified_basis` places a broker-order-sync Job at `base + 1 min` (a broker effect) and a clean account reconciliation at `base + 2 min`. With a wall-clock base both lie AFTER the real release, so (a) a SEEDED clean reconciliation would sit in the data of a module that promises none, and (b) after the new Job finishes at a wall-clock instant the newest clean reconciliation is the seeded future-dated one, so the plan's own expectation (gate `reconciliation_required`, "J4's effect is newer than the release") cannot hold.
- **Fix:** `evaluation(..., with_reconciliation=False, base=...)` with a `base` computed so that the sync Job (`base + 1 min`) lies strictly between the last broker effect (J1's reclaim, and for the recorded rejection the newest attempt start, i.e. the execution watermark) and the REAL release, and the evaluation's completion (`base + 3 min`) after it. The real release is then the clean standalone reconciliation of the basis window: the sequence sync, real M5, evaluation, Start of production. No reconciliation row is seeded anywhere in the new module.
