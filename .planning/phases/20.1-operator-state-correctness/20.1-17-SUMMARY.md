## TL-4 note

Legacy zero-attempt orders (no operation intent row, no attempt row) are never proven not sent, for `pending_submission`, `submission_failed` and `unknown` alike (D-12, S3-R4 last sentence). This replaces the former "gate-neutral" treatment of a legacy `submission_failed` order so that G2, the basis check, the predicate, A5 and the submit gate all give one answer.

## Real-data impact

Any real legacy zero-attempt order (`pending_submission`, `submission_failed` or `unknown`, no broker id, no operation intent row) now gates its strategy (`outcome_unresolved`), A5 (seeding, handover, release) and the Continue/submit gate until the supported sync/reconciliation flow resolves it: a broker lookup by client_order_id that finds and verifies it (plus the fresh clean reconciliation when its Job is flagged), or it stays unresolved (never found: no timeout, statement or reconciliation releases it). `run_paper_session` no longer binds such an order itself; recovery of it happens only in the broker-sync passes.
