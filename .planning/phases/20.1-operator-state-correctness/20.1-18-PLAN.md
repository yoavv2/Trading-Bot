<objective>
Close REVIEW SAF-10 (low: append-only and complete-once are code-only; the FK cascades evidence) and SAF-12 (low: session_run_lock cleanup can skip connection.close()).

Purpose: the safety property "never resend after ambiguity" must not rest on application discipline alone: a completed `ambiguous` attempt must be impossible to rewrite to `pre_connection`, and deleting an order must not silently erase its attempt evidence and turn its intent into a re-sendable `planned` one. Sequencing: wave 2 after 20.1-17 so the new trigger is exercised by the shared-classifier fixtures (which seed attempt rows) and never races them in a shared working tree.
Output: migration 0028 with a DB trigger and a RESTRICT FK, migration tests, and the nested-cleanup fix in concurrency_guard.py with a test.
