<objective>
Deliver COR-05 (D-07, D-08, D-11; R-3, D-1): attribution becomes evidence-based. Broker orders are classified owned / recorded_external / unrecognized with a closed origin tag, fills inherit their order's class, unexplained exposure per symbol is computed and blocks, and broker sync stops adopting positions (local positions derive from owned fills; broker positions are only compared). History never hides unresolved activity, and a page-cap overflow is a blocking unresolved result, never truncation.

Purpose: today any broker position the platform does not track is silently adopted by `_sync_positions_from_broker`, a platform-format id is trusted by shape alone, and a prior owner's orders would look like unknown activity to a new owner. Seeding, handover, account-level reconciliation, external-activity recording and recovery (20.1-08/09/10/12) all consume this classification.
