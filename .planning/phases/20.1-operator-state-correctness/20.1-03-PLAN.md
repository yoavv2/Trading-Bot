<objective>
Fix COR-01 at its source: a risk evaluation must not corrupt account truth (D-27, R-4, C-A). Evaluation stops persisting an `AccountSnapshot` (whose `buying_power` was set to cash, which made every later reconciliation diverge from a broker whose buying power is a multiple of cash); it records its portfolio basis on its own run instead. Cash for sizing and the reconciliation baseline both come from the latest broker-observed snapshot.

Purpose: the 29 Sep reconciliation blocked execution because the evaluation's derived snapshot became the "latest" local account state. No Sync-before-Trade workaround may be needed in the product (S3).
Wave 1 / depends_on []: no prerequisite plan. This plan owns `services/risk.py` (`run_risk_evaluation`), `services/portfolio.py`, `services/reconciliation/report.py` (baseline selection only) and `services/analytics.py` in wave 1; 20.1-04 (wave 2) later edits `services/risk.py` for `symbol_not_ready` and therefore depends on this plan (serialization of the shared file). This plan creates NO migration.
Output: `services/account_baseline.py` helper; basis-recording risk evaluation without snapshot writes; broker-observed baseline in reconciliation and analytics; replay, property and stage-order tests.
</objective>
