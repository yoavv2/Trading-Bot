<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions (locked)

### Ownership (PAPER-01, PAPER-02)
- **D-01:** At most one **active paper strategy** owns the Alpaca paper account. It's a persisted singleton; two owners are unrepresentable (DB constraint).
  - The four states are distinct: registered, available for research, enabled, active paper strategy.
  - Enabled ≠ active: disabling keeps ownership.
- **D-02:** The initial state is **no owner**. The operator explicitly selects the first strategy after the account checks pass. Trend following is never auto-selected.
- **D-03:** `paper-session` and **strategy-scoped** `reconciliation` for a non-owner are rejected at submit (`strategy_not_active_paper_strategy`); with no owner, `no_active_paper_strategy`. Ownership is re-checked immediately before every broker action.
- **D-04:** Seeding and handover checks (persisted evidence only; synchronous control; no broker call):
  - A1: no broker-touching Job queued or running, and no open operation;
  - A2: all broker orders terminal;
  - A3: flat, with 0 unexplained exposure;
  - A4: no unrecognized items;
  - A5: no unresolved outcome for any strategy;
  - A6: a fresh clean account-level reconciliation after the latest broker-touching Job;
  - A7 (handover only): outgoing owner disabled.

  The new owner starts **disabled**. Handover is unavailable when the account isn't flat.
- **D-05:** New strategies are created **disabled** (`ensure_strategy_record`).
- **D-06:** Ownership periods are audit context and a consistency check only. The owning strategy comes from local records (`PaperOrder` → run → strategy), never from time.

### Attribution, account checks, external activity (COR-05, ACCT-01, EXT-01)
- **D-07:** Broker orders are classified `owned` / `recorded_external` / `unrecognized`.
  - `owned` requires: a local intent registered before submission; matching `client_order_id` or `broker_order_id`; matching symbol, side, quantity and type; broker `created_at` ≥ registration; and the owner at registration.
  - `unrecognized` origin tags: `platform_format_unverified`, `external_format`, `status_unmapped`, `replaced_by_successor`.
  - An ID format alone is never ownership evidence.
- **D-08:** Fills inherit their order's class via `order_id`. Unexplained exposure = broker quantity − (owned + recorded-external net fills). Anything unexplained **blocks**, wherever it is in history. Broker sync **never creates positions** (no adoption).
- **D-09:** Owner-less **account-level** broker sync and reconciliation are always available: report-only, assigning nothing to any strategy, submitting nothing, lifting no gate by itself.
  - Results are stored in a **dedicated `account_reconciliation_runs`** record (architectural recommendation R-31), never attached to an arbitrary strategy (`strategy_runs.strategy_id` is NOT NULL).
  - Account snapshots use the nullable `account_snapshots.strategy_id`.
- **D-10:** **Record external activity** is a Job with a required reason.
  - It re-fetches verified broker records.
  - It requires every external order terminal and net external exposure zero.
  - It stores immutable snapshots with a content hash and preserved origin, with no strategy and no position.
  - It runs a fresh account-level reconciliation **in the same handler**; only that result can lift a block.
  - Every later check re-verifies recorded items.
- **D-11:** Page-cap overflow surfaces as `broker_history_exceeds_cap` (unresolved), never truncation.

### Submission and recovery (COR-06, REC-01)
- **D-12:** Order POSTs are never automatically re-sent on ambiguous failure.
  - Every HTTP attempt is logged durably, before and after the attempt.
  - Submission class over **all** attempts: `not_sent` only if every attempt is `pre_connection` (`ConnectError`, `ConnectTimeout`, `PoolTimeout`) or `deadline_expired` with complete outcomes. **Proven not sent** (round 5) requires positive evidence, recorded by the sending executor, that the request never left the process (zero attempts counts only for intents registered under the attempt log; legacy orders are never proven not sent); a missing outcome is uncertainty, not proof, and one proven-not-sent attempt never resolves an earlier or later ambiguous attempt. Any `ambiguous` (other transport errors, timeouts, 5xx, 429, missing outcome) → intent `UNKNOWN`, Job `outcome_uncertain`. A duplicate is recognized by the message "client_order_id must be unique" → lookup. Other 4xx → rejected.
  - GET lookups keep retries.
- **D-13:** Status mapping: `done_for_day` → working; `replaced` → terminal-with-successor (unrecognized successor); unmapped → `unknown` / `unmapped_broker_status`. `get_order_by_client_order_id` treats only HTTP 404 as not-found evidence.
- **D-14:** Recovery classifies each registered intent.
  - **Found and verified → resolved**, whatever its broker state.
  - **Not found → unresolved**, whatever the time elapsed or the absence evidence (W-1 declined).
  - ~~Only a recorded broker statement of non-receipt resolves a never-found order; it permits one resend.~~ **Superseded (round 5, 2026-10-04):** a broker statement of non-receipt is retained as audited evidence and neither resolves the order nor authorizes a resend, because it cannot show that a request that already left the process will not arrive later. A never-found order resolves only when the broker shows it (found and verified) or its attempt history proves it not sent; otherwise the strategy stays blocked with no product-level release (TL-4). End, expiry, re-evaluation, a new order version and ownership changes (A5) never bypass the block; recovery stays available after End.
  - `nothing_submitted` needs execution-path evidence: for `paper-session`, no linked `paper_execution` run; `broker-order-sync` never submits.
  - Resolution also needs a fresh clean **standalone** reconciliation (account or owner level; not the in-session check) after the latest broker-touching Job.
- **D-15:** **Supersedes Phase 20 D-19** (amended 2026-09-30):
  - **every** `paper-session` submission (fresh or retry) is rejected while an uncertain outcome for the strategy is unresolved (`outcome_unresolved` / `reconciliation_required` / `reconciliation_not_clean`);
  - a SUCCEEDED reconciliation alone never resolves;
  - `broker-order-sync` is never gated and never resolves by itself;
  - the orchestration layer calls a domain read predicate.

### Execution operation (REC-02)
- **D-16:** One execution operation per (strategy, evaluation session, pinned risk run), spanning several Jobs. At most one open operation per strategy (partial unique index). `risk_run_id: null` is resolved to a concrete id at the first submission.
- **D-17:** Sequential submission. The operation **pauses** after any broker result whose effects aren't accounted: a working order, or a filled order not yet synced (temporary limitations TL-1, TL-2). There is no same-operation exception.
- **D-18:** Closed states and reasons:

| State | Reasons |
|---|---|
| `paused` (unsent preserved) | working order / unsynced fills / awaiting reconciliation / kill switch / strategy disabled / reconciliation blocking / unrecognized activity / outcome unresolved / broker unavailable / before the open |
| `requires_reevaluation` (unsent frozen) | `evaluation_data_changed` / `strategy_settings_changed` / `risk_limit_failed:<code>` |
| `terminated` (**unsent only** → expired or cancelled) | `execution_window_elapsed` / `cancelled_by_operator` / `evaluation_superseded` |
| `completed` | — |

- **D-19:** **Continue session** is its own paper-session mode (`mode: continue`, `operation_id`) with its own Idempotency-Key. It is **not** an OPS-07 retry. It runs inside the (strategy, session) advisory lock.
  - Checks: terminal orders; owner sync after they finished; fresh clean standalone reconciliation; manifest match; per-intent permission and fresh risk check.
  - It skips submitted intents and never uses `create_new_version`.
- **D-20:** **End operation ≠ resolving uncertainty.** End (a synchronous control; refused while a Job of the operation runs) and expiry only terminate **unsent** intents. They never cancel broker orders, never resolve ambiguous intents, never erase recovery records or attempt logs, and never change trading permission. Recovery stays available after End.
- **D-21:** Termination on window expiry is **lazy**: applied on the next touch, since there is no scheduler.

### Calendar, execution policy, provenance (COR-04, PROV-01)
- **D-22:** Three separate facts, each with an explicit `unknown(calendar_data_unavailable)`, from the **persisted** calendar:
  - trading day (date + phase);
  - evaluation session (latest completed session with **ready** data: bars + warmup + calendar);
  - execution window (regular hours of `next_session(S)`, open until a configurable cutoff before close).
- **D-23:** The initial execution policy `regular_hours_prev_session_v1` is a named, versioned setting, **not** a platform invariant. Paper execution is eligible only when:
  - the trading day is open;
  - S is ready and equals `previous_session(trading day)`;
  - now is inside S's window;
  - the manifest matches.

  **Historical execution is rejected** (`historical_execution_rejected`); research, backtests and evaluation of past sessions stay available.
- **D-24:** `sync-market-sessions` may sync ahead to a configurable coverage horizon (default ≥ 60 sessions, bounded by the library); `calendar_runway_low` below a threshold. `ingest-bars` keeps `TO_DATE_IN_FUTURE`. Session-scoped submission defaults use the evaluation session, never "latest session with bars".
- **D-25:** The **evaluation input manifest** is stored in the risk run `result_summary` (no schema change).
  - It records every read request through the shared accessors (`bars_for_sessions`, `missing_bars_for_session`, `bars_for_session_date` valuation reads, calendar lookups with their **resolved as-of bound**), with result digests **including empty results**, plus the signal-settings digest.
  - Verification re-executes the requests **as recorded**. Only changed values, added or removed rows, or changed settings are stale; `updated_at` is never used.
  - A boundary test forbids direct `DailyBar`/`MarketSession` reads in strategy or risk code.
- **D-26:** **Provenance ≠ current portfolio risk.** Source data and signal settings are provenance. Cash, positions, open orders, earlier fills in the same operation, and risk-limit configuration are checked fresh before each order through `revalidate_pinned_intent`, with no new signals and no resizing. An earlier fill **never** triggers re-evaluation by itself.

### Account truth and outcomes (COR-01, COR-03)
- **D-27:** Risk evaluation persists **no** `AccountSnapshot`; it records its portfolio basis on its run.
  - The reconciliation baseline is the latest **broker-observed** account snapshot.
  - Sizing uses cash from it (never margin buying power).
  - Existing `risk_evaluation` snapshots are excluded by source.
- **D-28:** Batch operations report `complete | partial | failed` from the domain result, never on the Job model.
  - `sync-symbol-metadata`: per-symbol reasons (`not_found`, `missing_required_fields`, `invalid_response`, `fetch_error`) vs operation-level failures (auth, config, database, provider-wide) → `failed`. This replaces `raise_for_failures`.
- **D-29:** Required symbol metadata (`metadata_provider` set, `active`, `market='stocks'`, `symbol_type ∈ {CS, ETF}`, `primary_exchange`) gates per-symbol readiness. Not ready → `not_ready(missing_metadata)`; risk rejects its candidates with a new closed code `symbol_not_ready` (`risk_events.decision_code` is `String(64)`, so no migration).

### Interim operation and legacy console (COMPAT-01)
- **D-30:** New actions are **HTTP API only** until v1.4 (runbook: `05-INTERIM-API-OPERATIONS.md`, R1–R6 / M1–M16; M13 intentionally absent). All new mutating routes are ORCH-07-gated and added deliberately to the pinned mutating-route allowlist.
- **D-31:** Existing console adjustments (smallest truthful behavior):
  - `api_only` catalog marker: no paper-session start, no paper-session Retry;
  - an **Outcome** shown next to the Job status (a paused operation is never a plain success);
  - reconciliation and broker-sync shortcuts and forms default to `scope: "account"`;
  - the reconciliation panel never says "does not block execution" while trading is blocked;
  - a read-only "Active paper strategy" line.
  - No new pages, routes or controls. `tests/test_console_api_contract.py` stays green. API changes are additive.

- Table and column names beyond those stated.

## Planning-review amendments (2026-10-03, rounds 1–3; 04 "Planning review, round 3")

Approved behavioural amendments the plans implement (the price pause was held as PD-1 in round 6 and approved 2026-10-04):
- **D-18 closed sets:** paused adds `price_unavailable`, `price_moved_beyond_tolerance` (round 5; approved as PD-1 on 2026-10-04: moved from re-evaluation so Continue can send the same pinned intent once every Continue check passes again; ~~the alternative keeps it a re-evaluation reason~~ superseded) and `not_active_paper_strategy` (an operation whose strategy is no longer the owner; after SER only through direct DB writes; never sends). Re-evaluation reasons stay `evaluation_data_changed`, `strategy_settings_changed`, `risk_limit_failed:<code>`.
- **D-16 corollary:** `risk_run_already_operated` (409) when the pinned risk run's operation is `terminated`; a `completed` one returns `noop_existing_orders` (zero order submissions, no new operation).
- **D-10 addition:** refusal reason `order_owned_by_strategy`; already-recorded items are idempotent no-ops.
- **S1-R3 submission authority (20.1-11/15/16; amended round 5):** authority and the attempt row are committed in one transaction T1 before EVERY HTTP attempt (retries included). T1 authorizes only an intent that is proven not sent over its whole attempt history (`pre_connection` or `deadline_expired`, or zero attempts); an empty outcome, a recorded timeout or any other ambiguous result is uncertain. Takeover commits a new epoch, then treats every uncertain intent as in doubt. Guarantees, stated separately: (a) one durably recorded original request may reach the broker late — a platform boundary, not a duplicate; (b) while it may still produce an execution, no other request is authorized for the strategy — no retry, no new intent, no new version, under any operation, evaluation or owner. There is no resend path in this version: executor termination does not retract a sent request, and neither a non-receipt statement, absence evidence nor elapsed time proves the original cannot arrive. `previous_executor_terminated` is removed.
- **S2-R3 pre-send price (20.1-15; approved round 3; round-5 pause approved as PD-1 on 2026-10-04):** every pre-send risk check uses Alpaca latest trade (`trade.p`, `trade.t`) within a freshness window and a deviation tolerance; no fresh price pauses `price_unavailable`. A deviation beyond tolerance pauses `price_moved_beyond_tolerance` (next action `wait_for_price_then_continue`) and sends nothing. An explicit Continue may send the same pinned intent (same intent, client_order_id, symbol, side, quantity and identity; no replanning, resizing or new version) once the price is back within tolerance and every applicable check passes again: fresh price, permission (owner, enabled, kill switch, window, per-intent check), provenance (manifest), recovery (no unresolved earlier submission; intent unsent or proven not sent), reconciliation (orders terminal, sync, fresh clean standalone reconciliation), risk at the fresh price and the TL-10 allowance. Changed evaluation inputs or strategy settings still require re-evaluation; window expiry still terminates the operation under D-21 and price recovery does not override it; an unresolved earlier submission keeps blocking and Continue never resends an in-doubt intent. ~~*Alternative:* it remains a re-evaluation reason (End → re-evaluation).~~ *(superseded by the PD-1 approval)*
- **S3-R4 intent identity and action justification (20.1-15; amended round 5):** same intent (retry/Continue) never repeats its executed effect; a completed-evaluation replay returns `noop_existing_orders`. A new intent must pass, in order: (1) no unresolved submission of the strategy; (2) a verified basis (snapshot from a broker sync after the execution watermark that applied every earlier order's final state and ingested its fills, then a clean standalone reconciliation before the evaluation, positions matching; `evaluation_basis_unverified` otherwise); (3) no working order on the symbol; (4) not a replay — the timestamp-free decision fingerprint differs from every broker-reaching intent (`replay_of_earlier_decision`); the fingerprint is replay detection only and a changed fingerprint never proves a new intent; (5) the action is justified by the strategy's semantics against the verified portfolio (entry only when flat, exit only when held, exit quantity = the whole verified position); (6) the session allowance is not consumed (TL-10, `action_already_submitted`); (7) risk approval, revalidated at the fresh price. A new order version is created only when every earlier version is proven not sent (`version_bypass_refused` otherwise). For a never-sent intent a fresh evaluation may authorize the action without any data, settings or portfolio change; a settings change alone proves nothing but can still enable valid trading when (1)–(7) hold. Remaining strategy limitations: L-S1 a held symbol is never bought again; L-S3 (corrected) quantity and the deviation baseline come from the evaluation session's close, so if the fresh price stays outside tolerance for the whole window the action is not taken that session, and re-evaluating the same session helps only once the fresh price passes again. A legacy order (no attempt rows, no operation; e.g. Phase 20 orders) is never proven not sent.
- **SER ownership serialization (20.1-01/09/12/15):** the singleton row always exists (seeded `id = 1, strategy_id = NULL`; fail closed if missing). Job admission of every broker-touching type (FOR SHARE), handover (FOR UPDATE) and run-time operation creation (FOR SHARE) serialize on the active_paper_strategy singleton row.
- **R-Q1 (20.1-01):** single-statement gate loader used by `run_paper_session` and the operator reads; gate behaviour unchanged.
- **S4 code catalog (20.1-11):** every code belongs to exactly one category (submission refusal, execution block, execution refusal, pause, re-evaluation, termination, no-op, control refusal).

## Temporary limitations (accepted; see 04 §0)

- **TL-1:** repeated Continue within one session.
- **TL-2:** a working order blocks further orders.
- **TL-3:** single execution policy (regular hours).
- **TL-4 (amended round 5, 2026-10-04):** a found ambiguous order resolves normally; a never-found one blocks the strategy (and, through A5, every ownership change) until the broker shows the order or its attempt history proves it not sent. A broker statement of non-receipt is audited evidence only. There is no product-level release and no resend in the initial scope.
- **TL-5:** external activity only when terminal and net-zero.
- **TL-6:** handover only when flat.
- **TL-7:** full broker history re-read.
- **TL-8:** lazy window expiry.
- **TL-9:** API-only operation until v1.4.
- **TL-10 (round 5):** at most one broker-reaching action per (strategy, evaluation session, symbol, side) — an initial product limitation, not a permanent platform invariant. Consumed by any earlier intent with that key that reached or may have reached the broker — an attempt not proven not sent, broker evidence, or a legacy order without proof (broker-accepted in any later state including partial fills, broker-rejected at submission, or ambiguous); not consumed by planned, registered_unsent, proven-not-sent, cancelled_unsent or expired_unsent intents. A partially filled exit therefore leaves no second sell in that evaluation session even though a position remains. Unresolved submissions are subject to the stronger TL-4 block.
- **TL-11 (round 5):** partial-fill remainders are not pursued automatically. The remaining position is preserved, exposed (Sessions and Overview reads) and included in every current risk check. A partial buy is never topped up (all four strategies reject an entry for a held symbol). A partial-sell remainder is sold by a later evaluation session's exit: trend_following_daily and time_series_momentum_daily exit on the next session whose exit condition holds; donchian_breakout_daily and rsi_mean_reversion_daily hold it while their signal is flat and exit only when their exit condition fires again.
